# The lighthouse

The lighthouse at Saltby Point was built in 1874, on a spit of rock that the sea had been trying to reclaim ever since. For a hundred years a keeper lived there, climbing the hundred and twelve steps each evening to light the lamp.

The last keeper, a quiet man named Arthur Penhale, kept a logbook. Most entries were about the weather. Some were about ships. One, written in a storm in 1953, simply says: "Lamp lit. Holding."

# What came after

The lamp was automated in 1981. Today a computer in a city sixty miles away decides when it turns on, and nobody climbs the steps at all.

But the logbook is still there, on a shelf by the door. Visitors read it, and most of them, for some reason, end up reading that one line out loud.
